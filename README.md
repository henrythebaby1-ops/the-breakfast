# The Breakfast

A two-player paper-collage puzzle game. Breakfast pieces fly around the board. One cook presses **A** to stop a piece moving sideways, the other presses **L** to stop it moving up and down. Line every piece up with the pencil sketch, then watch a diner regular eat what you made.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repo.

## How to play

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `A` | Stop the piece horizontally (player one) |
| `L` | Stop the piece vertically (player two) |
| `Enter` / `Space` | Next order |

The on-screen A and L buttons work on touch screens too.

- **START** serves the full course of eight dishes: Sunny Side Up, Toast & Jam, Morning Coffee, Rice Bowl, Egg Rolls, Waffle, Cake Slice and The Tall Stack.
- Click a dish on the guest check to play just that one.
- **Easy** is slower and outlines the current piece in red. **Normal** is the diner way. **Hard** is faster, and in the last four dishes the pieces flash and jump around.

Everything is one HTML file. The art is drawn in SVG and the music is synthesized live with the Web Audio API, so there are no image or audio files.
